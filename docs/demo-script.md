# Demo Video Script (60–90 seconds)

## Tone
Confident, professional, approachable.

## Script

**0:00–0:10 | Introduction**
> "Hi, I'm G B Harish — a Software Engineer and Full Stack Developer. Welcome to my portfolio website, where I showcase the projects and skills I've built through hands-on experience and continuous learning."

**0:10–0:25 | Hero & About**
> "The site opens with an animated hero section that immediately communicates who I am and what I do — with clear call-to-action buttons and links to my GitHub, LinkedIn, and email. The About section highlights my professional summary, career objective, and quick stats."

**0:25–0:40 | Education & Experience**
> "My education timeline shows my B.Tech in Computer Science, diploma, and SSC achievements. Right below, the Experience section details my internship at Exafluence Global Analytics, with specific accomplishments and the technologies I used."

**0:40–0:55 | Skills & Projects**
> "Skills are organized into clear categories — languages, frontend, backend, databases, and tools. The Projects section features three key projects with technology badges, hover overlays, and a live demo link for my featured PQP Hub project built with React and Firebase."

**0:55–1:10 | Contact & Closing**
> "The contact section includes a working form powered by Formspree, plus direct links to my social profiles. The entire site is responsive, accessible, and built with semantic HTML and modern CSS — no JavaScript frameworks, just clean, performant code."

**1:10–1:20 | Outro**
> "Thanks for watching. Visit harishdevs.vercel.app to see the live site, or connect with me on LinkedIn and GitHub. I'm always open to new opportunities and collaborations."

## Production Tips

- **Screen recording:** Use OBS Studio (free) or Loom
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080
- **Framerate:** 30fps
- **Audio:** Use a USB microphone or phone earbuds with mic in a quiet room
- **Background:** Clean, well-lit environment if showing yourself
- **Pacing:** Speak slowly and clearly; use pauses between sections
- **Captions:** Add subtitles using YouTube's auto-caption tool or a tool like Kapwing
